# VarmanWAF v1.1.5 - API allowlist follows IP-group edits

**Release date:** 2026-10-22 **Images:** `sovichetra/varmanwaf:1.1.5`,
`sovichetra/varmanwaf:latest` **Tag:** `v1.1.5`

One production bug: the control plane's IP allowlist kept enforcing the
**previous** contents of a referenced IP group after the group was enabled or
edited, so an address that was visible in the group (and counted in the
"entries in the allowlist" badge) could still be refused until the settings
were saved again or the process restarted.

## What this round fixes

- **The protection policy now watches the referenced IP group too.** The
  background refresher rebuilt the in-memory policy only when the
  `api_protection_settings` row changed; group edits live on their own page,
  so enabling the "Cambodia (KH)" group after saving the allowlist left the
  old policy (inline ranges only) in force. The policy now tracks the
  referenced group's `updated_at` - including while it is disabled - and
  rebuilds when either input changes (`inputs_changed`). A disabled group
  still contributes nothing, and the log line says so.
- **A stable CIDR-membership test** pins the reported case: a `/22` from an
  ipverse country group (`116.197.152.0/22`) must contain `116.197.152.21`
  and not the next block - the matching itself was correct; the policy was
  stale.
- **Immediate workaround for the affected instance** (also recorded in the
  release notes of the fix): re-save the API protection settings once - the
  update handler rebuilds the policy synchronously - or restart the control
  plane.

## Verification at release

- `varman-control`: 6 protection tests including the two new ones -
  `an_ipv4_cidr_covers_addresses_inside_it` (the exact reported range) and
  `a_referenced_group_edit_rebuilds_the_policy` (group edit, group
  appearing/disappearing, settings save).
- Console: tsc clean, eslint 0 errors, vitest 25/25.
- Full 45-script E2E sweep on the deployed build.
- Live confirmation on the affected deployment: re-saving the settings
  rebuilt the policy (effective entries 1 → 660) and the client address in
  the group was then allowed.
