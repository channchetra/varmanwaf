01 Does my application have to be written in Rust?+

No. In all-in-one or split mode VarmanWAF proxies any HTTP backend. The security engine is native Rust, but your origin can be PHP, Node, Python, Go or anything else that speaks HTTP.

02 What is the difference between Monitor and Block?+

Rules detect and score; the site's enforcement mode decides what happens next. Monitor records every finding without blocking. Block answers matching findings at the edge. Policy tuning is the gate, and shared policies can carry it across sites.

03 What happens if the control plane goes down?+

Traffic keeps flowing. Every edge persists its last-good bundle, blocked IPs and config hash; losing the control plane stops changes, never traffic. Edges reconnect with backoff and sync when it returns.

04 Can I use OWASP CRS?+

Yes. CRS 4.30.0 runs on the built-in SecLang engine - no ModSecurity required. Rules the parser cannot compile stay visible with their error instead of being dropped silently, and a paranoia ceiling keeps noisy rules off the edge.

05 Is VarmanWAF free to use?+

Yes. It is Apache-2.0, self-hosted, with no per-request fees and no vendor lock-in. You run the control plane and the edges on your own infrastructure.
